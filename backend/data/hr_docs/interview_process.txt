Hiring and Interview Process
=============================

Job Posting:
- Open positions are posted on the company careers page and major job boards.
- Internal candidates are encouraged to apply and are given preference when equally qualified.
- Referrals from current employees are welcomed; the referral bonus is $1,500 paid after 90 days of the new hire's employment.

Application Review:
- The recruiting team reviews applications within 5 business days of submission.
- Qualified candidates are contacted for an initial phone screen.

Interview Stages:
1. Phone Screen (30 minutes) — conducted by a recruiter to assess basic qualifications and cultural fit.
2. Technical/Skills Interview (60 minutes) — conducted by the hiring manager or a team lead.
3. Panel Interview (90 minutes) — 3-4 team members assess competency and collaboration skills.
4. Final Interview — with department head or senior leadership for senior roles.

Interview Guidelines for Interviewers:
- All interviewers must complete unconscious bias training before conducting interviews.
- Questions about age, race, religion, national origin, disability, pregnancy, or marital status are prohibited.
- Use structured interview questions aligned with the job competencies.
- Submit interview feedback in the ATS within 24 hours of the interview.

Offer Process:
- HR extends verbal offers before sending written offer letters.
- Offer letters include: job title, start date, salary, bonus eligibility, and benefits summary.
- Background checks are required for all new hires before the start date.
- Drug screening may be required for certain roles.

Onboarding:
- New hire orientation takes place on the first Monday of each month.
- The first week includes IT setup, benefits enrollment, and department introductions.
- A 30-60-90 day onboarding plan is provided by the manager.
- New employees are assigned a buddy for the first 90 days.

Probationary Period:
- All new hires serve a 90-day probationary period.
- Performance is formally reviewed at 30, 60, and 90 days.
- The probationary period may be extended once for an additional 30 days.
