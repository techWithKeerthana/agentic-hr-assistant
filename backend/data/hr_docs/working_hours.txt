Working Hours and Schedule Policy
==================================

Standard working hours at Northwind Traders are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break, making the standard workday 8 hours.

Flexible Work Arrangements:
- Employees may request flexible start/end times between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM with manager approval.
- Core hours when all employees must be available are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
- Remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week for eligible roles, subject to manager approval.
- All remote work arrangements must be approved in writing by your manager and HR.

Overtime:
- Non-exempt employees who work more than 40 hours per week are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5x their regular rate.
- Overtime must be pre-approved by your manager.
- Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay.

Timekeeping:
- All non-exempt employees must record their hours using the company timekeeping system.
- Time sheets must be submitted every Friday by 5:00 PM.
- Falsifying time records is grounds for termination.

Breaks:
- Employees are entitled to a 15-minute paid break for every 4 hours worked.
- A 30-60 minute unpaid meal break is required for shifts longer than 6 hours.

Attendance:
- Employees must notify their manager at least 30 minutes before their scheduled start time if they will be absent or late.
- Chronic tardiness or absenteeism may result in disciplinary action.
- Three or more unexcused absences in a 90-day period may result in termination.
