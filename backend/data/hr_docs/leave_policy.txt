Leave and Time Off Policy
==========================

Paid Time Off (PTO):
- Full-time employees accrue 15 days (120 hours) of PTO per year in their first 1-3 years.
- After 3 years of service: 20 days (160 hours) per year.
- After 7 years of service: 25 days (200 hours) per year.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis.
- PTO begins accruing from the first day of employment.
- Maximum PTO carryover is 5 days (40 hours) into the next calendar year.
- Unused PTO above the carryover limit is forfeited at year-end.
- Upon termination, accrued unused PTO is paid out.

Sick Leave:
- Employees receive 5 days (40 hours) of paid sick leave per year.
- Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out upon termination.
- A doctor's note may be required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.

Holidays:
- The company observes 11 paid holidays per year:
  New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day,
  Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day,
  Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Day.
- If a holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest weekday is observed.

Family and Medical Leave (FMLA):
- Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year.
- Eligibility requires 12 months of employment and 1,250 hours worked in the past year.
- FMLA covers: serious health condition, birth/adoption of a child, care for a family member.
- Employees must give 30 days notice when the leave is foreseeable.

Parental Leave:
- Primary caregivers receive 8 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers receive 2 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption.

Bereavement Leave:
- Up to 3 days of paid bereavement leave for immediate family members.
  (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, in-law)
- Up to 1 day for extended family members.

Jury Duty:
- Employees are granted paid leave for jury duty for up to 10 days.
- Employees must provide a copy of the jury summons to HR.

How to Request Leave:
- Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned absences.
- For emergency leave, notify your manager as soon as possible.
- Manager approval is required for PTO requests.
