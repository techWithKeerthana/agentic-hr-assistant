General HR Policies
====================

Code of Conduct:
- All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism.
- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation are strictly prohibited.
- Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Equal Employment Opportunity:
- Northwind Traders is an equal opportunity employer.
- We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability,
  genetic information, sexual orientation, gender identity, or veteran status.
- All hiring, promotion, compensation, and disciplinary decisions are based on merit.

Anti-Harassment Policy:
- Sexual harassment, including unwelcome advances, requests for favors, and verbal or physical conduct
  of a sexual nature, is prohibited.
- Report harassment to HR or your manager immediately.
- All complaints are investigated promptly and confidentially.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment in good faith is prohibited.

Performance Management:
- Annual performance reviews are conducted in December for the prior year.
- Mid-year check-ins are held in June.
- Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: Needs Improvement, Developing, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, Outstanding.
- Merit increases are tied to performance ratings and are effective January 1.
- Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) receive a 60-90 day structured improvement plan with clear goals.

Disciplinary Process:
1. Verbal warning — documented in employee file.
2. Written warning — employee signs acknowledgment.
3. Final written warning — may include suspension without pay.
4. Termination.
- Serious violations (e.g., theft, violence, harassment) may result in immediate termination.

Resignation and Termination:
- Employees are asked to provide at least 2 weeks written notice of resignation.
- Final paychecks are issued on the next regular payday after the last day worked.
- COBRA continuation coverage information is provided within 14 days of termination.
- Return all company property (laptop, badge, keys) on the last day.

Expense Reimbursement:
- Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring expenses.
- Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.
- Meals with clients: up to $75 per person.
- Hotel: up to $200 per night in major cities, $150 elsewhere.
- Mileage reimbursement at the current IRS rate.

Workplace Safety:
- All workplace injuries must be reported to HR within 24 hours.
- Emergency evacuation procedures are posted at all exits.
- First aid kits are located in the break room on each floor.

Privacy and Data:
- Employee personnel files are confidential and accessible only to the employee and HR.
- The company may monitor use of company equipment and systems.
- Employees must not share confidential company information with outside parties.

Conflict of Interest:
- Employees must disclose any outside employment or business interests that may conflict with their role.
- Acceptance of gifts from vendors over $50 must be reported to HR.

Social Media:
- Do not share confidential company information on social media.
- Clearly state that opinions are your own when discussing work-related topics publicly.
- Do not make disparaging comments about the company, customers, or colleagues.

- Email: hr@northwindtraders.com
- HR portal: https://hr.northwindtraders.com
